# Vectren (CenterPoint Energy) (vectren)
Vectren Corporation was a Fortune 1000 energy holding company headquartered in Evansville (Newburgh), Indiana providing regulated natural gas and electric distribution services to customers in Indiana and Ohio. Vectren merged with CenterPoint Energy on February 1, 2019 in a $6 billion transaction. Vectren customer-facing portals continue to operate at vectren.com redirecting to CenterPoint Energy services.

## APIs

### CenterPoint Energy Builder Portal
Web portal for builders and developers in Vectren/CenterPoint Energy service territories in Indiana and Ohio. Provides self-service tools for ordering new gas and electric service, checking order status, requesting meters, and managing construction projects.
